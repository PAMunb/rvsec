## Why

The full-mode analysis document is mostly whitespace, and the distance horizon that bounds its pairs is fixed in code. Study 03's replication package re-analysed 40 apps in full mode on 2026-11-11 (image `phtcosta/rvandroid:0.9.6`, GATOR at `88d6e153`, `--full-output`). The measurements below were taken by that session and are reported here as it reported them:

- **Size.** The 40 full documents take 7.8 GB; the compact documents of the same 40 apps (E7) take 0.27 GB. Five apps account for 86 % of the total. `org.wikipedia_50595.apk` alone is 2.697 GB on disk and 0.388 GB minified (14.4 %). About 86 % of that file is the two-space indentation: an indented pair `[i, d]` spans four lines and about 70 bytes, against about 9 bytes without whitespace. Minified, its `reachability` holds 380 MB: 41,080,602 pairs over 5,207 targets and 15,603 methods with pairs. That app finished only in round D, with a 60g heap.
- **Horizon.** In wikipedia's pairs, `d ≤ 3` is 1.3 % of the total, `d ≤ 6` is 20.6 %, `d = 7` 16.3 %, `d = 8` 20.7 %, `d = 9` 22.0 % and `d = 10` 20.4 %. The peak at 9 and the 20 % still at 10 show that the horizon of 10 cuts off a tail of the distribution.

Neither is a defect. Both come from earlier decisions. gh125 kept the full mode indented on purpose: INV-ANA-86 requires it to be byte for byte the document the client wrote before gh125, so that published documents could be reproduced. `TargetDistances.DIST_MAX = 10` dates from gh120. The author decided on 2026-11-11 to change both.

The consumer of the distances, `aperv-tool`, has to follow, for three reasons:

- **It assumes the fixed horizon.** Its derive keeps the widget and handler pairs at `d ≤ DIST_WEIGHED_MAX = 3`. Calibration v6 (`rvsec-calibracao`, decisions D15 and D35, 2026-11-02) made that cut a parameter in its own copies of `tool.py` and `derive_mop_artifact.py` (`infra/v6/harness/`, commits `86c3cf9` and `667c371`). The copies started from the bytes this repository holds today (`tool.py` `5e75238c…`, `derive_mop_artifact.py` `42e8abf2…`). They bound the cut by 10, "GATOR's BFS horizon". Once the horizon is a parameter, that bound belongs to the document.
- **It is out of step with the jar.** The `ape` master (merge `a0fb5c16`, change `gh-tbd-mop-weights-list`) replaces `ape.mopWeightD1..D3` with the list `ape.mopWeights`, whose length is the cut, and it refuses the old keys with `retired_key`. The image builds the jar from that branch (`docker/rvandroid/Dockerfile:27-31`). This repository's `tool.py` still maps `mop_weight_d1..d3` to the retired keys. Its delivery note says so (`ape` `delivery-v6.md`, section 8).
- **The jar needs more heap.** The jar loads the whole MOP artifact into its heap. Calibration v6 measured that a deeper cut overflows the device's default growth limit of 192m on `org.wikipedia_50595.apk` (D53, 2026-11-03), and it started the APE with 512m.

## What Changes

The author's decisions of 2026-11-11, with his answers of the same day to the questions they left open.

**GATOR and `rv-static-analysis`:**

- **The full mode is written without whitespace.** `JsonReportWriter` stops setting the two-space indent. The full document keeps the same content: the same members in the same order, the same pairs, the same values. Only the whitespace between tokens goes away, so both modes are now serialised the same way. What tells them apart is the `distancePairs` marker and the pair reduction. INV-ANA-86 is amended from "byte-identical to the document written before gh125" to "the same content, without whitespace". The byte comparison against the old document (on `cryptoapp.apk`, identical through byte 68,750) becomes a comparison by content. **BREAKING** for any reader that depends on the full document's line layout. Every reader in the workspace parses JSON (`read_analysis_document` with `ijson`, the converter, the derive), so none is known to depend on it. A full document no longer reproduces the bytes of a published document. It reproduces their content.
- **The distance horizon becomes a parameter.** `RvsecAnalysisClient` reads `-clientParam distMax=N`. `N` bounds the reverse searches of the distance pass and of the distance-path pass (`TargetDistances.reverseBfs`). The default stays 10, so a run without the parameter writes the same content as today, the compact document included. With the parameter, a method's pairs can change in both modes. In compact mode, INV-ANA-85 keeps the 3 nearest targets even beyond `d = 3`, so a method whose targets are all far keeps different pairs under another horizon.
- **The document records the horizon only when the parameter is passed.** A run given `distMax=N` writes the top-level member `distMax: N` in both modes and in both writes (pre-WTG and final). A run without the parameter writes no such member, and its absence means 10. A reader of a full document can then tell a pair beyond the horizon from an unreachable target, without changing the default document.
- **An invalid horizon stops the run.** `N` must be an integer ≥ 1, with no upper bound. `rv-static-analysis` refuses any other value before it starts GATOR. GATOR itself, when a direct caller passes an invalid value, aborts the run with an error naming the value, and writes no document. This differs from `fullOutput` and `distancePaths`, which fall back to their default in silence on an unrecognised value. A silent fallback on a number would hand someone who asked for 15 a document computed with 10.
- **`rv-static-analysis` exposes the parameter.** `RVStaticAnalysisConfig.dist_max: Optional[int]` (default `None`, `≥ 1`) and `--dist-max N` on `analyze` and `batch` append `-clientParam distMax=N`. When the field is `None`, nothing is appended.
- **A stored document is reused only under its own horizon.** When `rv-static-analysis` finds an `<apk_name>.json` already on disk, it compares the horizon that document records with the one the run asks for. A missing member counts as 10 on both sides. When the two differ, it regenerates the document and logs both values, as INV-ANA-70 does for the scope key. It reads the recorded horizon from the start of the document and stops at the first member after the scope block, so a reuse check costs one read of at most 64 KiB (`ijson`'s buffer), not a parse of a document that can pass 2 GB. A stored document of the other output mode is still reused, as today.
- **The texts that fix the 10 follow the parameter**: the output-artifact line `1 ≤ d ≤ 10`, INV-ANA-73, "Per-Target Call-Graph Distance", the path-query requirement, `JsonSchema.java`'s comment, the client's log line, and the module documentation (`rvsec-gator/CLAUDE.md`, `rv-static-analysis/CLAUDE.md`, the `--full-output` help text).

**`aperv-tool`, brought from calibration v6 without its `cal` variant:**

- **The weighed cut is a parameter of the derive.** `derive(document, …, cut=DIST_WEIGHED_MAX)` keeps on each widget and handler list every pair at `d ≤ cut`. `activityDist` keeps its `DIST_K = 3` nearest whatever the cut. The cut is an integer from 1 up to the document's horizon: its `distMax`, or 10 when the document does not carry one. A cut above the horizon is refused, because the document holds no pair beyond it and the deeper levels would stay empty without notice. A `distMax` that is not an integer ≥ 1 is refused too, as the derive refuses any section of the wrong type.
- **The weight ladder replaces the three per-level weights.** The setting `mop_weights`, written `500;400;300` in the tool DSL (the DSL splits parameters on commas), becomes `ape.mopWeights=500,400,300` in `ape.properties`. `mop_weight_d1..d3` leave the mapping. `tool.py` derives the artifact at the cut max(3, length of the ladder). It refuses a compact document whose `distancePairs.weighedMax` is below that cut. It puts the cut in the cached artifact's file name when the cut is not 3, so an artifact derived at one cut is never reused at another. The ladder takes 1 to 10 non-negative integers, which is the range the jar accepts.
- **The APE starts with a 512m heap in every cell.** Before the launch, `tool.py` sets `dalvik.vm.heapgrowthlimit` to `512m` as root (`su 0 setprop`) and reads it back. If the property does not read back as `512m`, the task fails before the exploration starts. The app under test forks from the zygote, which read the property at boot, so it keeps its own heap.
- **The MOP cells stop stating the coin's ledger budget.** `mop`, `as_mop`, `as_mop_llm` and `mop_nolaunch` drop `mop_coin_ledger_max_interactions: 2`, so the coin runs at the jar's default. The author decided on 2026-11-11 that the MOP cells run the jar's calibrated defaults, and that this removal enters this change. The jar on the `ape` branch `gh-tbd-mop-defaults-f1` (`a867e471`) takes calibration v6's finalist F1 as the defaults of four MOP keys: the ladder `194,146,97`, retirement after 10, the ledger budget 4 and the launcher stagnation step 85. No cell states any of the four. An unstated key of a mounted feature takes the jar's default, so a MOP cell runs the ledger at 4 on that jar and at 1 on the master `a0fb5c16`, which is neither E7's 2 nor F1's 4. This `tool.py` therefore ships paired with the F1 jar, never with `a0fb5c16` alone. `mop_coin_ledger_max_interactions` stays in the mapping as a DSL ablation key, and `mop_nolaunch` keeps only `activity_trigger_enabled: False`.
- **The `aperv-tool` copy of the cryptoapp fixture is refreshed**, with its README, from the regenerated `rv-static-analysis` fixture.

**`rv-tools` and `rv-android-core`, for the `ape` built-in:**

- **Upstream APE starts with the same 512m.** The author's rule of 2026-10-21 is that `aperv:base` stays the APE. Under that rule the two tools already share the teardown grace (`tools` INV-TOOL-27). With 512m only in the aperv cells, `base` would run with more heap than `ape`, which keeps the AVD's default of 192m. The author decided on 2026-11-11 that `ape` gets the same 512m. The value and the property name become constants of `rv_android_core.constants`. The set and the read-back become one method of rv-android-core's `Android` class, and both tools call it before they start APE, so neither keeps its own copy.

**Not in this change:** a run on a device. The change is checked by unit tests, by the GATOR runs, and by offline checks against the code before the change and against the `ape` master's source. The heap setprop first runs on a device in the smoke of the next campaign. Also out: the calibration itself and its `cal` variant; the compact reduction (INV-ANA-85, `COMPACT_WEIGHED_MAX`, `COMPACT_K`) and the derive's defaults (`DIST_WEIGHED_MAX = 3`, `DIST_K = 3`), so INV-ANA-88 is untouched; choosing the horizon or the ladder for the next analysis or campaign, which the author has not decided; measuring the GATOR heap a larger horizon needs, which the author excluded (the pairs stay in the heap: 41 million in wikipedia at `d ≤ 10`); re-analysing or converting any corpus; building the Docker image; `rv-experiment`, which keeps the default horizon without a new option; reusing a stored document across output modes; the `ape` repository, whose cryptoapp fixture copy is the aperv session's (it is told when this change closes).

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `analysis`: five requirements are added: "Distance Horizon Parameter", "An Invalid Distance Horizon Stops the Run", "The Document Records a Given Horizon", "`rv-static-analysis` Passes the Distance Horizon" and "A Stored Artefact Is Reused Only Under Its Own Horizon". Five are modified. "Compact and Full Output Modes of the Analysis Document" writes both modes without whitespace. "Per-Target Call-Graph Distance" stops at the run's horizon instead of a fixed 10. "Distance Paths" repeats its searches with the same horizon. "Path Query Subcommand" says "within the document's horizon". "Full JSON Remains the Sole Metric Input" no longer says the two modes differ in whitespace. The `distMax` key enters `JsonSchema.Keys` and `_JK` under the existing rule of "Shared JSON Schema Keys" (INV-ANA-32), which needs no change.
- `aperv`: five requirements are added: "The Weight Ladder Setting", "Weighed Cut Follows the Weight Ladder", "A Document That Cannot Carry the Cut Is Refused", "The Cached Artifact Is Named by Its Cut" and "APE Heap Growth Limit". Seven are modified:
  - "ApeRVTool Execution Flow" sets the heap limit before the command and derives at the cell's cut, and the `as_mop` file loses its ledger line;
  - "Derived MOP Artifact Generation and Caching" derives at the ladder's cut and names the cached artifact by it;
  - "ape.properties Composition" maps `mop_weights` and drops `mop_weight_d1..d3`, and no cell writes `ape.mopCoinLedgerMaxInteractions`;
  - "MOP Artifact Projection and Handler Table" cuts the widget and handler lists at the cut instead of a fixed 3;
  - "ApeRVTool Configuration" drops the ledger line from the scenario of the platform's device addressing;
  - "ApeRVTool Cell Variants" drops the ledger budget from the three MOP cells and from `mop_nolaunch`;
  - "Cell Configuration Matches the Jar's Cell Echo" compares the MOP cells with their fixtures on every member, `digest` included, checks offline that the jar's ledger default is 4, and reads the `ape` checkout that `$APE_REPO` names instead of a named branch.
- `tools`: one requirement is added, "APE Built-in Starts APE With the APE-RV Heap Growth Limit".
- In the three specs, the text outside the requirements is edited by hand at archive (listed in `design.md`):
  - amended: INV-ANA-73, INV-ANA-86, INV-APV-47, INV-APV-64, INV-DRV-05 and INV-DRV-10;

## Impact

- **Modules:** `rvsec-gator` client (`JsonReportWriter`, `RvsecAnalysisClient` parameter reading and log line, `TargetDistances` horizon, `JsonSchema.Keys`, and the test replicas in `JsonOutputTest` and `SentinelEmissionTest`, which set the indent the writer no longer sets); `rv-static-analysis` (`config.py` field and command, `__main__.py` option, `_JK`, the reuse check in `analysis/static/static_analysis.py`, module documentation); `aperv-tool` (`tools/aperv/derive_mop_artifact.py`, `tools/aperv/tool.py` with the four MOP variants of `get_variants()`, their tests and the migration tier's ledger test, the cryptoapp fixture copy and its README); `rv-android-core` (the two heap constants in `constants.py` and `Android.set_ape_heap_growth_limit` in `util/android/android.py`); `rv-tools` (`builtin/ape/tool.py`, its test and README). `rv-platform`, `rv-coverage` and `rv-agent` are unchanged in code.
- **Cross-module interface:** the `.apk.json` contract changes in two ways. The full form loses its whitespace. When the parameter is passed, both forms carry one optional top-level member, `distMax`. `read_analysis_document`, `StaticAnalysisParser`, the parsed-copy cache and the converter read members by key and pass through the members they do not name. The converter copies `distMax` and still inserts `distancePairs` right before `distanceTargets`. `distMax` sits before both of them, so the converted document keeps GATOR's member order (INV-ANA-87). The derive reads `distMax` as its cut's upper bound.
- **Cross-repository pairing:** `ape.properties` written by `tool.py` uses `ape.mopWeights` and never the retired per-level keys, which is what the jar on the `ape` master accepts, and it states no ledger budget, which needs the F1 defaults. The jar that pairs with it is the one of the `ape` branch `gh-tbd-mop-defaults-f1` (`a867e471`), a child of the master `a0fb5c16` that adds the F1 defaults. The image builds the jar from the master (`docker/rvandroid/Dockerfile:27-31`), so it pairs once that branch is merged there. The pairing is checked offline by `aperv-tool`'s migration tier (`tests/migration`), run with `APE_REPO` on the source of `a867e471`, extracted with `git archive`: its cell gate and its mapping sweep read the jar's presets and key tables, and its ledger test reads the jar's default. With the ladder `500,400,300` stated in the plan the jar scores as the E7 jar does (`ape` `MopLadderE7EquivalenceTest`). Between `a0fb5c16` and `a867e471` the `RUN_START` fixtures of the MOP cells change only in `digest`, and with no ledger override the masked `RUN_START` of `mop`, `as_mop` and `as_mop_llm` is exactly its fixture.
- **Fixtures:** `modules/rv-static-analysis/tests/resources/cryptoapp.apk.json` (written in full mode) is first compared by content with the new jar's full document, which is the evidence for the amended INV-ANA-86. It is then regenerated from the new jar, as gh125 and gh-tbd-gator-distance-paths did, and the pins in `MANIFEST.json` are refreshed. Its byte copy in `aperv-tool`'s fixtures and that README's sha256 follow. The whole `tests/parity` suite runs after the jar changes.
- **Build:** the GATOR jar is rebuilt and installed into `lib/gator/`. The image is rebuilt outside this change. Both changes can enter the same build and the same image. This change and the merge of `gh-tbd-mop-defaults-f1` into the `ape` master reach the same image build: with only this change the MOP cells run the ledger at 1, and with only the F1 merge they run F1's ladder, retirement and cadence with the ledger at 2, a combination calibration v6 did not measure.
- **FR/NFR:** FR04/FR06 (GATOR output and distances), FR18/FR19 (APE-RV configuration and execution), NFR05 (configurable horizon and ladder), NFR08 (the full mode now reproduces published content, not bytes).
