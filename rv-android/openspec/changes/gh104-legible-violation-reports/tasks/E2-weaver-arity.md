## The rule (verbatim source: `docs/20260815_javamop_mensagens_FINAL_analise_lacunas.md:674-684`)

1. Absence of an `args()` clause means "no positional constraint" — never filter, never treat as length 0.
2. Read the length of `ArgsPC.types()`, not `names()`; a trailing `..` means "at least".
3. Filter in the grouping loop of `WrapperEmitter` — the only place advice and concrete overload coexist and `cc.paramFqns.size()` is known.

## Reach of the rule — what it does NOT cover (task 4.5)

This rule is partial **by construction**, and the partition below goes into `evidence/e2_reach.md` verbatim. Write it: a counter named `advicesExcludedByArity` invites the reader to assume every arity-incompatible advice was excluded, and that is false.

Two facts decide the reach. First, `ArgsPC` is referenced only inside `pointcut-engine` (`ArgsPC`, `PointcutExpression`, `NegationPC`, `PointcutMatcher`, `PointcutExpressionParser`) — the entire `advice-emitter` package, `BeforeEmitter`/`AfterEmitter`/`AfterReturningEmitter`/`AfterThrowingEmitter` included, never reads it. Second, `PointcutMatcher.matchArgs` returns `Match.empty(ap)` unconditionally when `!ap.hasTypeConstraint()`, and the binding form (`args(alg)`, `args(alg, *)` — every position a binding name or `*`) is exactly that case, so nothing constrains those advices at match time either. Your filter sits in the grouping loop, which admits only `shouldWrap(a)` = `"after".equals(a.getPosition())` (`WrapperEmitter.java:161-163`) and drops constructors with an explicit `continue` (`:251-256`).

The frozen descriptor's 115 advices therefore partition as:

| class | count | covered by this rule? |
|---|---|---|
| `after`, non-constructor, with `args()` | **48** | ✅ yes — this is what the grouping-loop filter sees |
| any position, no `args()` | **44** | — clause 1: never filtered, unaffected |
| `before`, with `args()` | **9** | ❌ no — bypasses wrappers; declared non-goal |
| `after`, constructor, with `args()` | **14** | ❌ no — dropped to the inline path by `:251-256` |

The 9 `before`: `CipherSpec_i1`, `CipherSpec_i2`, `MacSpec_i1`, `SecureRandomSpec_next1`, `SecureRandomSpec_next2`, `SignatureSpec_i1`, `SignatureSpec_i2`, `SignatureSpec_i3`, `SignatureSpec_i4`.

The 14 `after`-on-constructor: `DHGenParameterSpecSpec_c1`, `GCMParameterSpecSpec_c1_3`, `GCMParameterSpecSpec_c1_4`, `IvParameterSpecSpec_c1`, `IvParameterSpecSpec_c2`, `KeyPairSpec_c1`, `PBEKeySpecSpec_f1`, `PBEKeySpecSpec_f2`, `PBEKeySpecSpec_c1`, `PBEParameterSpecSpec_c1`, `PBEParameterSpecSpec_c2`, `SecretKeySpecSpec_c1`, `SecretKeySpecSpec_c2`, `SecureRandomSpec_c2`. Typical shape: `call(public DHGenParameterSpec.new(int, int)) && args(primeSize, exponentSize)`.

**Record this consequence, it is the one that matters for the change as a whole:** four of those fourteen — `IvParameterSpecSpec_c1/c2` and `PBEKeySpecSpec_f1/f2` — carry the **four non-`@fail` three-argument report sites** that Group 7 turns into envelopes (`IvParameterSpec.mop:48,55` and `PBEKeySpecSpec.mop:24,30`). After Group 7 those reports name their event and their observed value and are still raised by an advice this rule would have excluded. Legible and unfiltered is a real state, and a reader who sees `advicesExcludedByArity > 0` next to a legible `IvParameterSpecSpec` report must be able to find out from the evidence file that the two are unrelated.

Closing this at its root is the binding-form check in `PointcutMatcher` (`:268-306`), which is out of scope here and recorded as a follow-up question in `design.md`. Do **not** open it inside this group: it changes where every advice matches, not just which advices a wrapper fires, and it has no red test in this change.
