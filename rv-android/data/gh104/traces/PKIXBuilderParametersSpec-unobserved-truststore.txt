# THE CONFORMING HALF IS NOT EXPRESSIBLE, and the reason is worth writing down because it looks
# like an omission. It needs a key store that (a) holds a trusted entry, or the constructor
# throws, and (b) was observed being loaded, or `KeyStoreSpec` writes no `generatedKeyStore`.
# The notation cannot give both: `truststore` satisfies (a) but arrives without an observed
# `getInstance`, so its `load` falls outside the automaton and draws KEYSTORE-ORDER-00; and a
# store obtained through an observed `getInstance` is empty, because a line with no binding
# never reaches the platform and so never loads anything into it. Measured, gh109 task 7.3.
