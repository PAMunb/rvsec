# gh105 task 4.1: the update path, which is the only one that reaches the second
# acceptance point. `updates` leads to s3, where the `after updates` clause of
# api30 Cipher.cryptsl:189 is ensured, and the doFinal that follows reaches the
# accepting state, where the other two clauses are. Neither write is visible in
# an accusation until task 5.3 wires the `!encrypted` read of Mac, so what this
# trace measures here is the ordering: the pre-image drops the init out of the
# automaton and accuses the update, the successor accuses the init and lets the
# rest of the sequence through.
bind k = new SecretKeySpec(bytes, "AES")
Cipher.getInstance("AES/CBC/PKCS5Padding") -> c
c.init(1, k)
c.update(bytes)
c.doFinal()
