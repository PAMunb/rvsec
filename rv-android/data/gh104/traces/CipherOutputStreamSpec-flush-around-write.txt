# gh109 task 1.R, the conforming half of R4. Every flush a program may legally make, at every
# position it may make it, still passes: the repaired `ere` is `c1 fl* ((w1 | w2) fl*)+ cl`,
# which quantifies the `+` over writes and leaves flushes free around them.
Cipher.getInstance("AES/CBC/PKCS5Padding") -> cipher
new CipherOutputStream(null, cipher) -> cos
cos.flush()
cos.write(bytes)
cos.flush()
cos.close()
