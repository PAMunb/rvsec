## 6. Threats to validity of this consolidation

1. **Evolving round standards (D-batchA-1 → D-batchD-1).** Scoring
   presentation, FEN linkage, severity letters and evidence admissibility were
   tightened round over round. Cross-round comparisons of raw scores or claim
   counts are therefore **not** like-for-like; this report never pools scores,
   and pools claim counts only with the per-phenomenon lens attached.
2. **Pilot severity and FEN linkage are consolidator-derived.** The pilot
   record carries neither a severity column nor FEN ids; the ledger's 10 pilot
   criticals are a labeled parse of prose, and only 9 pilot claims are joined
   to canonical FENs (each with a cited source). Risk: under-linkage of pilot
   phenomena in the registry (pilot's Cipher/GCM findings largely stay
   pilot-local). Mitigation: nothing depends on pilot counts; every assert
   binds to A–D published figures.
3. **Alias-map risk.** Batch A and cross-round canonicalization are the
   consolidator's (batch B's map is the judge's own; C/D were pre-unified).
   Mitigation: asserts force the critical groupings to reproduce each round's
   published (critical-claims, critical-FEN) pair exactly; non-critical
   merges (e.g. UNKNOWN-EXPECTING→FAIL-UNKNOWN) follow the batch B map and are
   listed in `ALIAS` for inspection. Residual risk sits in non-critical rows
   only.
4. **Fail-open family membership (§1.3) is a judgment call** mandated but not
   enumerated by D-batchC-1. The selection rule and the excluded adjacent
   families are printed; batch A BETA-SET-03's refutation-tested mitigation is
   preserved beside its flag.
5. **Single-half G5 in A/B and dimension-5 route coverage.** Declared gaps the
   consolidation inherits rather than repairs: pilot exercised no dimension-5
   claims (pilot §8.4); batch A dimension-5 was single-route for 4/5 specs
   (REF-B-09) — and the one dual-route spec (HMC) is where single-route claims
   were wrong; batch C's declaration came only at refutation (REF-D-08),
   with Alfa's KST dimension-5 statements erratum'd; batch B/D were
   multi-route. Any set-level lifecycle conclusion leaning on A's four
   single-route specs carries that weakness.
6. **Dimension-5 routes aside, dimension coverage is uneven**: `toolchain`
   SET claims concentrate in Beta; `reprodutibilidade` has 6/34 INCONCLUSIVE;
   several units scored with unattainable weight (D-batchA-1 labels carried).
7. **Historical unit mismatch.** The MDG pairing cross-check (0.873 here vs
   2 780/2 871 quoted) uses a different cell definition; both are printed,
   neither asserted, no attribution rests on either.
8. **This consolidation re-executed nothing dynamic.** It consumes closed,
   refutation-tested records and re-verifies only what is checkable statically
   (hashes, CSV arithmetic, spec/rule/register text, predicate sites). The
   ART/device half, G8 corpus and G10 battery remain open inputs, and nothing
   here narrows them.
